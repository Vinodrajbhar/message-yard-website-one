Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a developer to launch a campaign? ↓
No. Marketing Cloud's Journey Builder and Campaign Manager are built for marketers — no code required. Developers only get involved when you want something custom via the CPaaS API.
How is this different from a marketing platform that "integrates with" a CPaaS provider? ↓
Those platforms resell someone else's messaging infrastructure. MessageYard owns the CPaaS layer directly — so deliverability, compliance, and pricing aren't dependent on a third party.
Can I bring my own LLM for Conversational AI agents? ↓
Yes. Agents can run on MessageYard's managed models or connect to your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or self-hosted model via API.
What compliance certifications do you hold? ↓
SOC 2 Type II, GDPR-ready, HIPAA-eligible on Enterprise plans, and CAN-SPAM/TCPA compliant messaging controls built in.
Is there a free trial? ↓
Yes — Starter is free to begin, with pay-as-you-go CPaaS usage and no credit card required.
